# Edge Thesis

## v1 Focus

- Auto Mode is intentionally limited to `official_event` markets.
- These markets have clearer resolution sources and lower ambiguity than narrative or macro-policy markets.
- The expected edge window is short enough that fast, human-readable shadow analysis is useful even without execution.

## Why Manual Mode Exists

- Macro-policy and narrative-driven markets need broader interpretation.
- Fed and policy questions can still be analyzed, but only in Manual Mode with wider uncertainty and lower confidence by default.
- v1 is designed to make that uncertainty explicit instead of pretending those markets are easy to automate.
